P09ELECTROMECHANICAL · TROUBLESHOOTING
IO113 / PROTECTION

GRUNDFOS S-SERIES · FIELD APPLICATION

Pump Protection & IO113 Integration

A technical application case focused on monitoring interfaces, sensor integration, cable requirements and operating conditions.

IO113 integrationSensor & cable reviewOperating point
View technical case ＋

Application overview

Grundfos S-series submersible pump application involving IO113 monitoring and protection integration.

Recorded site observations

  • Thermal-switch protection was not enabled.
  • A water-in-oil sensor was not present in the inspected arrangement.
  • Installed power and control cable lengths were recorded as insufficient for the site requirement.
  • Sensor-cable routing was noted as susceptible to electromagnetic interference.
  • The pump operating condition was reported at the upper end of its operating range rather than at the best efficiency point (BEP).

Technical review and recommendations

The recorded follow-up included reviewing the protection configuration and sensor provision, specifying a 25 m power-cable requirement, and changing / rerouting the sensor cable to address interference risk. The operating point was identified for review against the intended duty and pump curve.

Engineering disciplines

Pump protection, motor thermal protection, instrumentation, sensor integration, cable selection and routing, operating-point assessment and commissioning support.

Evidence boundary

This case documents observations and technical recommendations. It does not claim that every corrective action was completed or that a quantified performance improvement was achieved.
